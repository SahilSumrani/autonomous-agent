# Evidence-Backed Personalization

## Levels of Personalization

### Level 1 — Basic
- Utilizes company name, industry, discovery source, and generic service offering.
- Used only when no website research or specific post observation exists.

### Level 2 — Contextual
- Utilizes a specific public observation (post, reel, announcement) or presence of a website/product catalog.

### Level 3 — Deep Personalization
- Combines specific verified public observations with technical website findings (CMS, mobile navigation friction, conversion issues) and a tailored value proposition.
- Required for high-intent prospects.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Claims must map to observable DOM/HTML evidence or confirmed intent signals.
- Phrases implying unverified personal relationships or fake funding are rejected.
